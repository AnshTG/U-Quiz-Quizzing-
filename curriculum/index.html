Class 10 Science (13 Chapters)

CBSE Code 086

Chapter 1: Chemical Reactions and Equations

Balancing chemical equations, combination, decomposition, displacement, double displacement, redox reactions, corrosion and rancidity.

Chapter 2: Acids, Bases and Salts

Indicators, pH scale, chemical properties of acids and bases, preparation of sodium hydroxide, bleaching powder, baking soda, washing soda, Plaster of Paris.

Chapter 3: Metals and Non-metals

Physical and chemical properties, reactivity series, formation and properties of ionic compounds, basic metallurgical processes.

Chapter 4: Carbon and its Compounds

Covalent bonding in carbon compounds, versatile nature of carbon, homologous series, nomenclature of carbon compounds, ethanol and ethanoic acid.

Chapter 5: Life Processes

Nutrition in plants and animals, respiration, transportation in human beings and plants, excretion in plants and animals.

Chapter 6: Control and Coordination

Tropic movements in plants, introduction to plant hormones, control and coordination in animals, nervous system, reflex action, endocrine glands.

Chapter 7: How do Organisms Reproduce?

Asexual and sexual reproduction, reproduction in flowering plants, human reproductive system, reproductive health and family planning methods.

Chapter 8: Heredity

Heredity and Mendel’s contribution, laws for inheritance of traits, sex determination in humans.

Chapter 9: Light – Reflection and Refraction

Reflection by curved surfaces, image formation by spherical mirrors, mirror formula, refraction, laws of refraction, refractive index, lens formula, power of a lens.

Chapter 10: The Human Eye and Colourful World

Refraction through a prism, dispersion of light, atmospheric refraction, scattering of light (Tyndall effect).

Chapter 11: Electricity

Ohm’s law, electrical resistance, resistors in series and parallel, heating effect of electric current, electric power, interrelation between P, V, I and R.

Chapter 12: Magnetic Effects of Electric Current

Magnetic field lines, field due to a current-carrying conductor, solenoid, force on a current-carrying conductor, Fleming’s Left-Hand Rule, domestic electric circuits.

Chapter 13: Our Environment

Eco-system, food chains and webs, ozone layer depletion, waste production and management, biodegradable and non-biodegradable substances.

Class 10 Mathematics (14 Chapters)

CBSE Code 041

Chapter 1: Real Numbers

Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic, proofs of irrationality of √2, √3, √5.

Chapter 2: Polynomials

Zeros of a polynomial, relationship between zeros and coefficients of quadratic polynomials.

Chapter 3: Pair of Linear Equations in Two Variables

Graphical method of solution, algebraic methods: substitution and elimination methods, consistency conditions.

Chapter 4: Quadratic Equations

Standard form ax² + bx + c = 0, solutions by factorisation and quadratic formula, nature of roots (discriminant).

Chapter 5: Arithmetic Progressions

nth term of an AP, sum of the first n terms of an AP, real-life word problems.

Chapter 6: Triangles

Basic Proportionality Theorem (Thales Theorem) and its converse, criteria for similarity of triangles (AAA, SSS, SAS).

Chapter 7: Coordinate Geometry

Distance formula, Section formula (internal division), collinearity and geometric shapes.

Chapter 8 & 9: Introduction to Trigonometry & Applications

Trigonometric ratios of acute angles (0°, 30°, 45°, 60°, 90°), identities (sin²θ + cos²θ = 1), heights and distances.

Chapter 10: Circles

Tangent to a circle, radius-tangent perpendicularity, lengths of tangents from external point.

Chapter 11: Areas Related to Circles

Area of sectors and segments of circles with central angles 60°, 90°, 120°.

Chapter 12: Surface Areas and Volumes

Surface areas and volumes of combinations of cubes, cuboids, spheres, hemispheres and right circular cylinders/cones.

Chapter 13 & 14: Statistics and Probability

Mean, median and mode of grouped data, classical definition of probability, simple problems on single events.
