Q1 • Chemical Reactions & Equations ✓ Correct Key: Option B

Which of the following processes represents a chemical decomposition reaction requiring thermal energy?

A) Burning of natural gas
B) Heating of ferrous sulphate crystals (FeSO₄·7H₂O) [Correct Key]
C) Reaction of quicklime with water
D) Rusting of iron in moist air
NCERT Concept Rationale: NCERT Class 10 Ch 1

When green ferrous sulphate crystals (FeSO₄·7H₂O) are heated in a dry boiling tube, they first lose water of crystallization to turn white. Upon further heating, anhydrous FeSO₄ decomposes into solid ferric oxide (Fe₂O₃), reddish-brown residue, along with pungent gases sulphur dioxide (SO₂) and sulphur trioxide (SO₃): 2FeSO₄(s) → Fe₂O₃(s) + SO₂(g) + SO₃(g). This is a classic thermal decomposition reaction requiring heat absorption.

Q2 • Life Processes (Human Physiology) ✓ Correct Key: Option B

In the human digestive system, bile juice produced by the liver performs which primary function?

A) Digestion of proteins into amino acids
B) Emulsification of large fat globules and providing an alkaline medium [Correct Key]
C) Conversion of starch into maltose
D) Absorption of water in the large intestine
NCERT Concept Rationale: NCERT Class 10 Ch 5

Food coming from the stomach is acidic and must be made alkaline for the pancreatic enzymes to act. Bile juice secreted by the liver accomplishes this. Additionally, fats are present in the intestine in the form of large globules which makes it difficult for enzymes to act on them. Bile salts break them down into smaller globules (emulsification), increasing the efficiency of pancreatic lipase.

Q3 • Electricity (Ohm's Law) ✓ Correct Key: Option C

According to Ohm's Law, what happens to the electric current flowing through a conductor if the resistance is doubled while the potential difference remains constant?

A) Current is doubled
B) Current remains unchanged
C) Current is halved [Correct Key]
D) Current becomes four times greater
NCERT Concept Rationale: NCERT Class 10 Ch 11

Ohm's Law states that V = I · R, where V is potential difference, I is current, and R is resistance. Rearranging for current yields I = V / R. When the potential difference V is maintained constant, electric current I is inversely proportional to resistance R (I ∝ 1/R). Therefore, doubling the resistance (R' = 2R) results in I' = V / (2R) = I / 2, halving the current.
