Pedagogy & Curriculum Integrity

About the U Quiz Platform

U Quiz is engineered to eliminate rote memorization by providing high-quality, conceptual multiple-choice questions grounded strictly in the National Council of Educational Research and Training (NCERT) textbooks.

Strict 2026-27 Rationalization

Every prompt sent to our AI model is constrained by NCERT rationalized content guidelines. Dropped chapters (like Periodic Classification or Sources of Energy) are omitted, ensuring students never waste time on unexaminable material.

High-Speed AI Question Generation

Powered by Google's Gemini Flash Lite model with structured JSON Schema output. Questions generate in under 3 seconds with guaranteed 4 unique options, verified correct answers, and thorough conceptual explanations.

Privacy and Accessibility

U Quiz respects learner privacy. All user profiles and quiz histories are protected by Firebase Firestore security rules, allowing students to learn without invasive tracking or advertisements.
